# Mukusho Karate Kenya and Benard Kihachu Research Report

This report provides a detailed overview of Mukusho Karate Kenya and its lead instructor, Benard Kihachu, based on web, TikTok, and Facebook scraping.

## 1. Mukusho Karate Kenya Overview
Mukusho Karate Kenya is a prominent martial arts organization based in Kenya, primarily operating in the **Nyeri** region and expanding to surrounding areas like **Nanyuki, Murang'a, and Othaya**. The organization focuses on training children and youth in both sport karate and self-defense.

### Key Information:
*   **Mission:** "Empowering every child with the greatness within them through confidence, discipline, and teamwork."
*   **Location:** Headquarters in Nyeri, with sessions at locations such as Othaya Catholic Parish Hall and ACK St. James Cathedral Murang'a.
*   **Activities:** Kids' karate classes, individual and team kata, kumite, and participation in national and regional championships.
*   **Major Event:** The **4th Edition of the Mukusho Karate Kenya Children Championship (2026)** is currently being promoted as an upcoming major event.

## 2. Benard Kihachu (Ben Ben)
Benard Kihachu is the lead instructor and a central figure of Mukusho Karate Kenya. He is a highly accomplished martial artist who represents Kenya on the international stage.

### Professional Background:
*   **Occupation:** Martial Arts Coach and Instructor.
*   **Competitive Achievements:** 
    *   Member of the **Kenya National Karate Team**.
    *   Participated in the **Region East Karate Championship in Addis Ababa** (December 2025), competing in the **Male Team Kata** category.
    *   Also competed in the 11th Commonwealth Karate Championships in Durban, South Africa (Nov-Dec 2024), where the Male Team Kata stood on the podium.
*   **Experience:** Over 10 years of experience in coaching martial arts, specifically kids.
*   **Collaborations:** Involved in a girls' empowerment program in Kenya.
